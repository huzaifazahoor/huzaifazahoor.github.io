Service

Stock Screener Development

Custom screeners with your filters, your indicators, and your data source. I have built 15+ of these for the US, Nigeria, Nepal, Canada, and crypto markets.

A stock screener sounds simple until you build one. The data is messy. Tickers change. Splits and dividends break your history. APIs rate-limit you at the worst time. I have hit every one of these problems before, so you do not pay me to learn them on your project.

What you get

  • Technical filters: RSI, EMA/SMA crossovers, MACD, volume spikes, candlestick patterns.
  • Fundamental filters: P/E, market cap, dividend yield, earnings dates.
  • Real-time or end-of-day data, depending on your budget and data source.
  • Watchlists, saved screens, custom scoring, and CSV export.
  • Deployed on AWS or GCP with scheduled data updates.

Screeners I have shipped

Topchor, a full screener for the Nigerian Exchange with 145+ stocks and 20+ filters. NEPSE Research, a screener and pattern detector for the Nepal market. Bravo Scan, a US market screener with advanced insights. Plus Finviz-style screeners, options screeners, and crypto screeners built for private clients.
