Trading Bot & Alert Development

You have a strategy. I turn it into a bot that watches the market for you and pings you the moment your conditions are met.

Most traders lose good setups because they cannot watch the market all day. A bot can. I build bots that scan your watchlist on your schedule, run your exact rules, and send alerts to Discord, WhatsApp, or Telegram in seconds.   • Signal generators for stocks and crypto using technical indicators like RSI, EMA crossovers, MACD, and volume spikes.
  • Discord community bots that post buy signals, insider transactions, upgrades, and downgrades into your channels automatically.
